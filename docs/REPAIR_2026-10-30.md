# Macquarie count-format and technology-program repairs

## Verified defects

Production `c145618` reported 72 consecutive failures for `macquarie-campus`:
`Macquarie result count missing`. A permitted October 30 request using the
existing HTTP/robots policy returned eight actual campus records. Avature's
dedicated `list-controls__text__legend` displayed `8 results`, with whitespace,
instead of the range form `1-9 of 31 results` required by
`finance_sources.finance_avature`. Job identities, article markup and employer
detail/application destinations remained usable. A focused regression failed
on the old parser before repair.

The repair retains the range form and also accepts a single dedicated result
legend containing only an integer and `result(s)`. Arbitrary body text, missing
or conflicting legends and challenge pages do not become authoritative totals.
Declared-count reconciliation, complete pagination, duplicate/identity checks,
host restrictions and page bounds remain in force. The regression exercises a
short page, pagination, duplicates, changing totals, old-format compatibility,
challenge pages and conflicting counters.

The recovered Houston record, employer requisition `24477`, is titled
`2027 Technology Summer Internship Program`. It explicitly places interns in an
engineering team and describes software/application development and deploying
code, but the classifier rejected its generic technology title as outside the
role families. A second regression reproduced that false exclusion. The narrow
fallback now requires that generic program title, the employer's own
responsibilities section, an engineering-team assignment and concrete software
or code duties. Qualifications alone do not establish the role. Possible
placements remain ambiguous, IT helpdesk/qualification-only examples do not
qualify, and mixed hardware duties still require review. User filters and
eligibility rules are unchanged.
