## Queue capacity and monitoring

- Add configurable bounded parallel validation (`validation_workers`, 1–4,
  example setting 2). Separate HTTP sessions share existing per-host rate limits,
  robots decisions and cooldowns. Database writes remain on the main thread.
  The shared wall-clock budget stops new work; completed in-flight checks are
  retained, and unstarted checks retain their previous evidence/due time.
- Recognize repeated, unchanged Workday identity/content mismatches for the
  existing longer unverified recheck interval. First failures, transport errors,
  rate limits, server failures and unknown errors keep short retries.
- Queue warnings require 30 minutes of healthy observations before recovery.
  A lag incident opens above two hours, and recovery starts below one hour.
  Growth detection permits small dips; an open incident requires net non-growth
  before recovery. The recovery window persists across supervisor restarts and
  resets on relapse. Critical outage alerts are unchanged.
